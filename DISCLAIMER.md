# Appendix D — independent-prototype disclaimer

> **Independent experimental prototype**
>
> Trusted GOV.UK Knowledge Discovery is an independent, static and page-scoped experiment. It is not part of GOV.UK, is not an official UK government or local-government service, and is not endorsed by any public body or publisher. Its two evidence-presentation tools may change only reversible in-memory page state; no tool changes canonical metadata, browser storage, network resources or external state.
>
> Records are provided to help discovery. They may be incomplete, stale or wrong. Inclusion in a catalogue does not mean an API or dataset is publicly accessible, approved for your use, or covered by a particular licence. Access and licence are shown only where the cited source supports them.
>
> “Verified” means that the prototype’s declared metadata and digest checks passed. It does not mean that government has verified the record or that the underlying source is factually correct.
>
> The `0.3.0-rc.1` working candidate separates 80 item-reviewed records from 58,655 locked raw rows in four checksum-bound source snapshots. Exactly 3 standalone HM Land Registry legislation rows are quarantined, leaving 58,652 searchable federated records. The larger raw total is before cross-source deduplication and does not mean 58,655 unique, current or officially verified government resources. Federated records have snapshot and file-integrity evidence, not the reviewed tier’s item-level receipts. Their links and assertions are producer-declared, source authority is not independently established, and producer wording cannot make them official.
>
> HM Land Registry contributes public-estate metadata only; no title, ownership, address, polygon or personal row is included. UK Legislation is explicitly excluded as a standalone collection, payload, index and runtime request. The searchable projection contains no `legislation.gov.uk` result link. Source-authored strings in the locked raw metadata remain untrusted cross-references, not an admitted legislation collection.
>
> Inspect the linked page and its displayed destination hostname, and use an appropriate authoritative source before acting. Do not enter personal, confidential, official-sensitive or security information.
