Controlling deadline
The rules state the closing point as “September 3rd, 2026 (1:00 pm Pacific Time)”.
On 3 September 2026 Pacific Time is PDT (UTC−07:00) and UK civil time is BST
(UTC+01:00). Conversion: 13:00 PDT + 7 hours = 20:00 UTC; + 1 hour = 21:00 BST.
The plan therefore uses 3 September 2026 at 21:00 BST as the controlling deadline.
