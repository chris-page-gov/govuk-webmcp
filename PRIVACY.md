# Privacy notice

## What this prototype processes

Search terms are processed in the browser against a same-origin static
catalogue. They are not added to the URL, written to browser storage, sent to a
government API or deliberately logged by the application.

The application does not use accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising,
tracking pixels or persistent server-side storage. Its WebMCP tools accept only
bounded search and record inputs; they do not request unrelated conversation,
identity or personal context.

## Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages delivers the static files and may process ordinary request data
under GitHub's own privacy terms. Following an authoritative source link leaves
this prototype; the destination organisation's privacy and cookie notices then
apply.

Do not enter personal, sensitive or unpublished information into the search
box. The catalogue is a research fixture and is not a channel for contacting a
public body.
